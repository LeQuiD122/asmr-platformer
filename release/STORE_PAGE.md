## Description

Squish, squelch and pop your way across Harrow Bay!

Every step lands on something satisfying: slime that wobbles, honey that pulls at your feet, kinetic sand that keeps your footprint, bubble wrap that pops under you. Run five levels, from the City Shore to the Sky Pools, the Sunken City, the Flooded Halls and Hill House, and follow the bay's story to find out who you really are.

- Soft, satisfying ASMR sounds on every material
- Chill or Hardcore, short or long runs
- Collect pals, trails, auras, footprints and emotes
- A squish room with every material to play with
- A daily chest, daily challenges and secrets to find

## What badges do

A badge is a Roblox achievement. It does nothing inside the game; it shows on the game's page (with how many players have it and how rare it is) and on each player's profile. Players chase them, which keeps them playing longer and coming back, and rare ones are shown off. The game already awards all ten at the right moments; they are just not created on Roblox yet.
